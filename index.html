About Me

My name is Wenray Zhang. I am a 11th grade student attending Hillcrest High School and a member of the IB Program. My parents, two older brothers, and I live in Sandy, Utah. Both of my brothers are graduates from the University of Utah and we have a cat named Sesame who is a very friendly three month old domestic longhair.

Here at Hillcrest High, I am the president of the Chinese Club and officer for the Future Business Leaders of America (FBLA) Club. I have competed in FBLA for two years, qualifying for nationals both times. This year I even placed 7th nationally for my event, Website Coding and Development. I also compete in the University of Utah Science and Engineering Fair (USEF), placing 3rd state wide, and am a member of the USEF Student Advocacy Board.

My hobbies include reading, crochet, drawing, and lockpicking. I got into lockpicking because I wanted something tactile to do with my hands that involves a lot of problem solving as well. I also wanted to learn about physical security in a more interesting way than reading a lot of boring articles. Obviously, I don't pick other people's locks (unless requested). Not only is it illegal, but picking someone's rusted padlock is way more frustrating than picking a brand new lock. Lockpicking is also an extremely inefficient way to get into somewhere. There's a reason why it's not a popular skill, though I wish it was more popular. People's naivety when it comes to locks/physical security in general is shocking.
